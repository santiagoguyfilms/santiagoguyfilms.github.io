Stage 02
The Grinder
"I'm posting, but it's random and nothing sticks."

You're trying. You're posting. Maybe you have a website but it's outdated. Your Google reviews are thin. Your social media is sporadic — one week active, next week nothing. No consistent brand, no hooks, no calls to action that actually drive business. The effort is there. The structure isn't.

The Upside

+ You're already creating — that's the hardest part
+ You have content to audit and learn from
+ Small tweaks to hooks and CTAs can unlock big results
+ Your audience already knows you exist — now make them act

The Reality

! Inconsistency kills trust — online presence looks unreliable
! No automation means you're doing everything manually
! No strategy means you're busy but not building
! Scattered effort trains platforms to ignore you
Find Out If This Is You →
Stage 03
The Performer
"I'm active, I show up, but views don't equal customers."

You're not afraid to turn the phone around. You're posting consistently, maybe even running ads. But likes don't pay rent. You're getting attention but not converting it into bookings, sales, or leads. You don't need more activity — you need strategy, sharper messaging, and systems that turn attention into revenue.

The Upside

+ You have the content muscle most businesses never develop
+ Your audience is already watching — now direct them
+ Fine-tuning the message can 2-3x your conversions
+ You're one strategy shift away from a content machine

The Reality

! Views and likes are vanity metrics without a funnel behind them
! Activity without a client journey is entertainment, not marketing
! Your competitors are starting to copy what works — stay ahead
! Burnout hits hard when effort doesn't convert to revenue
