By using our website at www.santiagoguyfilms.com, engaging our services, or communicating with us via phone, email, or SMS, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We may collect the following types of personal information when you interact with us:

  • Name — your full name or business name
  • Phone number — mobile or business phone number
  • Email address — personal or business email
  • Business information — company name, industry, website, social media handles, and project details relevant to the services you request
  • Mailing address — if required for invoicing or service delivery
  • Payment information — processed securely through Stripe and PayPal; we do not store credit card numbers on our systems
  • Communication records — emails, SMS messages, and other correspondence related to service coordination

We collect this information when you:

  • Fill out a contact form or booking page
  • Purchase a service through our website
  • Provide your phone number for project coordination
  • Communicate with us via email, SMS, or phone
  • Schedule a call through our Calendly booking system

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following business purposes:

  • Service delivery — to provide the services you have purchased or requested
  • Contractor coordination — to connect you with qualified trade contractors and coordinate project logistics
  • Service quotes and estimates — to request, receive, and communicate quotes for projects
  • Appointment scheduling — to schedule and confirm meetings, kickoff calls, and project dates
  • Business communications — to send project updates, reminders, confirmations, and follow-ups
  • Invoicing and payments — to process payments and send invoices
  • Customer support — to respond to your questions and requests

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. Your data is never sold, rented, or traded to any third party for any purpose.

We do NOT share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share your information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — trusted platforms we use to deliver our services (e.g., Stripe for payments, Twilio for SMS, Google Workspace for email, Calendly for scheduling). These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and are only given the information necessary to perform their function.
  • Contractors — if you engage our contractor coordination service, we may share your name, project details, and address with qualified contractors for the sole purpose of providing quotes and performing work you have approved.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or governmental request.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encrypted payment processing through Stripe and PayPal (we never store credit card information)
  • Secure, access-controlled cloud storage for business records
  • Limited access — only authorized personnel can access personal data
  • Secure communication channels for sensitive information

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to industry-standard protections.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Active client data — retained for the duration of our business relationship and any active project engagements
  • Completed project records — retained for up to 3 years after project completion for warranty, support, and legal compliance purposes
  • Invoicing and payment records — retained for 7 years as required by tax and accounting regulations
  • SMS communication logs — retained for up to 2 years for quality assurance and dispute resolution
  • Contractor coordination records — retained for up to 3 years after project completion

After the applicable retention period, your personal data is securely deleted or anonymized. You may request earlier deletion of your data by contacting us (see Section 9).

  • Opt-out of SMS — reply STOP to any message to stop receiving SMS communications
  • Withdraw consent — you may withdraw your consent to data processing at any time by contacting us

To exercise any of these rights, email us at santiagoguyfilms@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
