Southern Homestead 2013 Reserve sit third at 2-0-1, unbeaten, with nine scored and only four conceded in three games: a 7-0 win away at Athletum 2013B Gatti, a 3-2 win over Weston FC 2013 Boys Blue, and a 2-2 draw away at SK 13-Gold. They also hold games in hand on Pinecrest, who have already played six.

The common opponents line up neatly. Both sides beat Weston Blue by the same 3-2 score. Against SK 13-Gold, Pinecrest won a 5-3 shootout at home, while Homestead were held to a 2-2 draw away. Pinecrest outscore teams; Homestead shut them down. Saturday is where those two ideas collide.

Keys to the game: be clinical, because four goals conceded in three games means chances will be rare. Bring the goals against number down, because Pinecrest's seventeen conceded is the figure that has kept them from pulling clear in second. And respect the unbeaten run without fearing it: Homestead have scored in every game they have played, so a clean sheet cannot be the whole plan.
