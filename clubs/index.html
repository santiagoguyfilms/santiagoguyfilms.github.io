The Club Hub

Every team. Every table. Every week. · by Santiago Guy Films · Miami, Florida

The Club Hub gives every team at your club its own page: standings, results and fixtures pulled from the official league data, a scouting report on the next opponent, and a weekly preview show your families can listen to on the way to the game.

It runs itself. Nobody at your club uploads anything, types anything, or maintains anything. We pull the league data, build the pages, record the show, and publish, every single week.

What Every Team Gets

A page of its own

Every team at your club gets its own page: record, league table, results and upcoming fixtures, living at your club's own web address.

The table, always current

Standings, results and fixtures are updated weekly from the official league data your club already plays in. No spreadsheets, no screenshots, no chasing.

Scouting report

Before every match, a written report on the next opponent: their record, their recent form, their goals for and against, and where the game will be won.

La Previa

A weekly preview show for every team, hosted by Sofia and Jeff, breaking down the coming match the way the pro game gets covered. Ready to share with your families before matchday.

How It Works

Step One

We plug into your leagues

Your club already plays in leagues that publish the data. We connect your hub to those official sources. Your staff does nothing.

Step Two

Every page updates weekly

After each round of games, tables, results, fixtures and scouting reports refresh across every team page at the club.

Step Three

Families get a show

La Previa lands before matchday for every team, ready to post in the group chat and share with your parents.
