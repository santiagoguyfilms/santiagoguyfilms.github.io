West Pines sit fifth in Bracket A at 1-3-0, and their season has swung hard in both directions: a 10-0 win over Florida Royal FC U10 NOVA, a 4-1 loss to PPO Heat Strikers, and a 5-0 loss away at the leaders, Miami Stars Homestead Lions, who have won four from four.

Hollywood have already seen this opponent up close. The first meeting at Boggs Field on September 13 finished 5-2 to Hollywood, part of a perfect home start: two played at home, two won, thirteen goals scored. The flip side is the away record, played two and lost two, including the 3-5 defeat at SK U10 Girls on October 17.

Keys to the game: start fast and score first, the way Hollywood did in September. Keep the game tight after scoring, because West Pines trade punches and have found the net in three of their four games. And lean on the attack: sixteen goals in four games, five of them against this exact opponent.
