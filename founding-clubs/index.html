The Club Hub · Founding Clubs · by Santiago Guy Films · Miami, Florida

The Club Hub gives every team at your club its own page: record, league table, results and upcoming fixtures, a scouting report on the next opponent, and a weekly preview show called La Previa. It is in full operation today at South Kendall Sunblazer Soccer Club, all 11 teams across three leagues, updated every Sunday night during the season from the official league data.

We are opening the product to ten founding clubs. Founding clubs join at introductory pricing and keep that pricing for as long as they stay members. When the ten places close, standard pricing applies to every club that follows.

What Every Team Gets

A page of its own

Every team gets its own page with its record, standings, results and upcoming fixtures, updated weekly from the official league data your club already plays in.

Scouting report

Before every match, a written report on the next opponent: their record, their recent form, their goals for and against, and where the game will be won.

La Previa

A weekly preview show for every team, with two hosts breaking down the coming match the way the pro game gets covered. Ready to share with your families before matchday.

Your colors, one link

Every page wears your club colors and your crest, at one link your families can open anywhere. No app to install, nothing for parents to learn.
